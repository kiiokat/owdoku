🎥 Streamer vs Chat

Race your Twitch chat for the grid. You pick a square, then you both try to name a player who fits. Chat just types names, and steals the square when two chatters name the same correct player. Most squares wins.

How are answers determined?

Players can be found by their current name or an old one.

How come a team or player is missing?

The game only covers these four leagues:

Teams from other leagues aren't in the game, and for OWCS only teams that reached top-tier events. A player needs a recorded roster spot or match with one of these teams. The game relies on Liquipedia, so a gap in a roster there shows up here too.

Credits

Player data

OWL hours and top heroes come from the official Overwatch League Stats Lab match records, 2018 to 2023 (2023 through June). OWL teams, seasons and roles are checked against Liquipedia rosters, and Liquipedia wins where the two disagree, which also fills in the rest of 2023.

OWCS teams (2024–2026) count players from top-tier OWCS events and OWCS-era rosters, APEX teams count everyone on their roster during OGN APEX (2016–2018), and Contenders teams count everyone who played for them at a Contenders event (2018–2023). OWCS, APEX and Contenders rosters, roles and signature heroes, plus every player's nationality and old names, come from Liquipedia, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

OWdoku has no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics. Here is everything it stores and sends.

On your device

Your boards, streak, rank, achievements and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing this site's data resets them.

When you finish a daily

The game sends the puzzle date, the nine answers on your board and a random ID that your browser made up. The ID only makes sure each browser counts once per day. It isn't linked to your name, email or anything else about you, and clearing site data gives you a new one.

These totals power the pick percentages and rarity scores. Practice and archive games send nothing.

Services the site uses

Cloudflare hosts the site and the stats server that stores the daily totals. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to deliver pages and may keep standard request logs.

Google Fonts serves the fonts, so your browser connects to Google when the page loads.
