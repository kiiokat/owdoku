How to play

  1. Fill each square with an Overwatch League player who fits both its row and its column. For example, a square under "OWL Champion" in the London Spitfire row needs someone who won a title and played for London.
  2. Any player who played in an OWL match from 2018 to 2023 counts, even if they only played a few maps.
  3. Each player can only be used once per grid.
  4. A wrong guess is a strike. After 3 strikes, the game ends and the remaining answers are revealed.
  5. After the daily puzzle, see what share of players picked each of your answers. Your rarity score adds those up (plus 100 per missed square), so lower means more unique picks.
  6. A new grid arrives every day at midnight. Missed one? Tap the calendar button at the top to play past grids from the Archive. Practice mode has unlimited random grids.

On each card: the player's most-played hero, their country flag (where known), and their role badge: Tank DPS Support
